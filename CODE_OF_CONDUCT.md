# Code of Conduct

DrukView is a civic project about public money. People will disagree about numbers, and sometimes about what
the numbers mean. That is the work. How we disagree is what this document is about.

## What we expect

- **Argue with documents, not with people.** "This figure is wrong, here is the Budget Report page" is the
  whole method. "You are biased" is not a correction.
- **Assume the other person is trying to get it right.** Most wrong figures here are transcription slips, not
  agendas.
- Be patient with newcomers, with people writing in their second or third language, and with people who know
  the subject but not the code.
- Accept review. A maintainer asking "which document says that?" is doing their job, not doubting you.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments — including about someone's nationality, ethnicity, religion,
  language, gender, sexuality, disability, or political views.
- Publishing private information about anyone, or using this project to target an individual. Criticism of a
  public body or of an office-holder's official acts is legitimate; a campaign against a person is not.
- Knowingly adding a false or uncited figure, or removing a citation. This is the one kind of bad faith the
  project cannot absorb, and it leads to an immediate ban.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, and ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages — and anywhere someone is
representing DrukView.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the repository owner through a GitHub direct message, or by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Youngyang07/DrukView/security/advisories/new) if you would
rather use a channel that is not public. Reports are read by the maintainers only.

We will respond, say what we decided, and keep the reporter's identity confidential. Responses escalate as
needed: a private word, a public correction, removal of a comment, a temporary block, a permanent ban.

Maintainers who do not follow this document are held to it like anyone else.
